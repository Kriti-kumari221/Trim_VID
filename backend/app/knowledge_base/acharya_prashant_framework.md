# Acharya Prashant Philosophical Framework & Editorial Knowledge Base

## Core Tenets & Editorial Truths for Viral Shorts / Reels

### 1. The Starting Point: Suffering (Dukkha) & The Sufferer
- Never start with cosmic theories, rituals, or distant philosophy.
- The entry door is always the viewer's immediate felt dissatisfaction: restlessness, ambition, loneliness, boredom, feeling incomplete.
- The fundamental inquiry: Not *"What am I suffering from?"*, but **"Who is the one who suffers?"**
- The diagnosis is the **Ego (Ahamkar)**: not arrogance, but the subconscious identification *"I am this, I am that"* (hopping from one identity/object to another).

### 2. The Illusion of Completion (Horizontal vs. Vertical)
- **Horizontal Movement**: Chasing more wealth, better jobs, relationships, degrees, or even spiritual techniques and retreats. Accumulating more objects on a flat plane.
- **Vertical Movement (Freedom)**: A lighter center. Dropping what is false.
- The core delusion: Hoping that horizontal accumulation will somehow add up to vertical height.

### 3. Vidya vs. Avidya (Inner vs. Outer Education)
- Outer Education (Avidya / worldly knowledge): Gives instruments, technologies, money, degrees, power.
- Inner Education (Vidya / self-knowledge): Knowing the instrument-holder.
- Disastrous consequence: Without self-knowledge, power and technology are put in the service of blind desire ("colonize Mars while destroying your own rivers and hills").

### 4. True Change Formula
- **Change = Seeing + Intent**
- Seeing alone changes nothing (one can see the falseness and live it for 20 years).
- Intent is not willpower (willpower is the ego fighting external symptoms). Intent is the ego deciding against itself.
- **The Price**: What dropping the false actually costs. Love is measured by the price paid.

### 5. Hook Archetypes & Retention Triggers for Shorts
1. **The Disturbance Hook (The Mirror)**:
   - "When a sentence disturbs you, stay with it; the disturbance is the point."
   - Exposing the hidden hypocrisy in ordinary life (marriage, career, validation).
2. **The Counter-Intuitive Truth**:
   - "Dullness is given, delusion is chosen."
   - "Suffering is the one credential the ego cannot fake."
   - "Whatever is personal is hell."
3. **The Trap of Hope**:
   - Exposing the next job/relationship as the old dissatisfaction wearing a new face.
4. **Compassion vs. Mercy**:
   - Mercy consoles the false self; compassion cuts the root of bondage.
